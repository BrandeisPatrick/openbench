# Reasoning-pattern constructs in the literature, mapped to the openbench directions

Compiled 2026-09-03 (round 2). Status: **design guidance, not findings.** Every statement about a paper is restricted to its adversarially verified record; refuted claims were dropped and corrected claims are used only in corrected form (ledger in §3h). Every statement about our corpus is a prediction or an operationalization, never a result. This document goes beyond [docs/literature-map-post-training-signatures.md](../docs/literature-map-post-training-signatures.md) and [docs/findings-verification-2026-08.md](../docs/findings-verification-2026-08.md) by answering the researcher's question directly: *which named reasoning-pattern constructs exist, which survive contact with agentic coding traces, and what each one predicts for D1–D6 on our three channels.*

**What changed since round 1.** Five literatures that the round-1 critique found missing are now integrated with verified records: (i) agentic-RL "thinking vs doing" training dynamics and the labs' own efficiency statistics (§1c, §2-D4, §3c); (ii) CoT faithfulness / monitorability / language-mixing / summary-fidelity results that bound what the raw-CoT channel can support (§1d, §3d, §4a gates); (iii) SWE-bench Verified contamination and memorisation probes as pre-registered covariates for D2/D3 (§1e, §3e); (iv) mode-collapse and diversity literature from RLVR, distillation and preference tuning, giving D2/D3 explicit rival mechanisms with signed predictions (§1f, §3f, §4b); (v) small-N statistics for trajectory comparison (§1g, §3g, §4b). Fourteen weak mappings named by the critique were rewritten or demoted; each carries a grounding tag: **V** = published construct computable verbatim on our channels; **X** = our extension of a published construct (named as such); **B** = name borrowed, the published construct is *not* what we can compute at our N.

Channel vocabulary: **CoT** = `reasoning_content` per API turn (raw for DeepSeek and Kimi; provider **summaries** only for OpenAI); **ACT** = executed shell commands with outputs; **PATCH** = final diff (+ reconstructable intermediate working-tree states); **GRADE** = test-based pass/fail. Lineages: DS = r1-0528 → v3.2 → v4-pro; OA = o1 → o3 → gpt-5.5; KM = k2-thinking → k2.6 → k3; g1/g2/g3 = generation index. One standing caveat inherited from the map and not re-verified here: the labs report that V4's and K3's distillation teachers are in-house specialists, not the prior public release; every "distillation inheritance" prediction below is conditioned on that.

## 2. Direction-by-direction mapping

Each row: named literature pattern → paper → operationalization on **our** channels → prediction per lineage (design hypotheses only), with grounding tag **V / X / B**. "No reasoning-content construct" is stated where the literature offers nothing CoT-side.

Two bounds apply to every CoT-side row below and are not repeated per row. **Verbalization bound**: in the hint paradigm a decision-relevant fact is verbalized 25% (Claude 3.7 Sonnet) / 39% (DeepSeek R1) of the time overall and 20% / 29% for misaligned hints, exploit-type decisions <2% in 5/6 RL environments ([Anthropic](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.05410)); unverbalized adoption is higher for tool-return cues than user cues on all 15 open models tested, including DeepSeek V4 Pro and Kimi K2.6 ([FACE-Eval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29464)). Our decision-relevant evidence arrives almost entirely through tool returns, so *absence* of a CoT mention is near-uninformative; presence is the only usable evidence. **Necessity bound**: CoT is computation rather than rationalization only where removing it costs accuracy ([Emmons et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.05246), Gemini-only, ≥50 pp uplift criterion); on four saturated tasks the gap is probably small, so CoT-content claims on the frozen corpus are rationalization-dominated by this criterion until the no-thinking control (§4b) is run.

**Contamination covariate protocol (pre-registered, must precede any D2/D3 interpretation).** (a) Two-condition file-localisation probe: "Ticket only" and "Ticket + File Structure", 5 samples × 9 models × our 4 tasks, metrics "% all gold files" and "% ≥1 gold file" ([Verified tests memory?](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.10218v2)), with the same probe on post-release sympy/pytest controls from [SWE-rebench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20411) and [SWE-bench Live](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23419); the Verified-minus-control ratio is the covariate ([SWE-Bench Illusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12286) reports 60–76% Verified vs ≤53% external for GPT/Claude families). (c) Patch-level memorisation screen: Jaccard over added lines and difflib ratio against gold, looking for the "lump near 1.0" ([SWE Atlas](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.08366)), plus session-isolated Contamination Score CS = 0.5ḡ + 0.3(1−div) + 0.2(1−σ_g) computed over our K repeats ([Cross-Context Verification](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.21454v1)) — note their astropy-7606 case is a memorised **non-gold** solution, the exact shape of D3, so the cross-model modal share must be the denominator before calling it memorisation. (e) Pre-register that a null on all of these is **not** evidence of no contamination: log-prob detectors collapse to chance after GRPO while inflation persists ([Fragility of Contamination Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02386)), and we have no logprobs at all.

**(f) What agentic RL does to these quantities during training (the closest published analogue to a within-lineage generation trend)**

- Interaction scaling beats per-step scaling: prompting a Gemma 3 12B web agent to "check again" moved WebArena 23% → ≥28% while equal compute on per-step budget forcing gave <3%; TTI's curriculum beat fixed h=10 by 5.7% and fixed h=30 by 19.6%; during training tokens per action *slightly decrease* while rollout interaction length first decreases then increases ([TTI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07976)).
- Redundant tool use *grows* under plain GRPO: tool calls ~320→>370 and response length ~2,300→>2,500 tokens by step 300 on Qwen3-4B ([AKBE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26952)); tool-use rate stays a flat 20–35% minority under GRPO and reward shaping does not close the gap ([AXPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28774)); tool abuse costs 18–25% of calls with no accuracy benefit ([EAPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02132)); "more tool calls do not necessarily improve performance" ([EAPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02132)).
- Efficiency-targeted recipes reverse it: OTC-PO cuts tool calls up to 68.3% and raises tool productivity 215.4% vs Search-R1-PPO ([OTC-PO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.14870)); ReTool's length drops ~40% (10k→6k) with code ratio →98% and earlier invocation ([ReTool](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.11536)); DeepSWE's per-step thinking drops sharply while environment steps rise ([blog](https://www.together.ai/blog/deepswe)); ASTER's first RL stage systematically reduces tool invocations to a lower plateau ([ASTER](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01204)).
- Cap handling matters for our v3.2 arm: masking over-length trajectories removes the negative examples of looping, so the agent "is not penalized for this looping behavior" ([Nebius](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.03501)) — a first-party reason cap-hit runs are not a uniform truncation.
- Void turns and rollout collapse are the named pathologies at the two ends: partial/empty turns under multi-turn RL ([SimpleTIR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.02479)) and 93.4% of entropy-guided branches concentrating on 1–3 trajectories with 56.5% consecutive high-entropy tool-call turns ([AEPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.14545)).
- Uncertainty is concentrated in the first 10–50 tokens after each tool return ([ARPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.19849)); with no logprobs, our proxy is the lexical/semantic novelty of the first sentence of reasoning after each command output (DS/KM only) — v3.2's grind predicts low post-output novelty.

**(h) Corrective results that constrain how D4 is read**

- Length growth under GRPO is partly an optimisation artefact of the 1/\|o_i\| term ([Understanding R1-Zero](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20783), "may also be attributed"); never read volume as "more thinking"; report length-normalised densities and solve-conditioned volumes only.
- Sample accuracy is non-monotonic in length while mode accuracy keeps rising; length bias is method-specific (GRPO Sample Avg, DRPO) and each curve point is a different policy ([Optimal Reasoning Length](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09591)).
- Unfaithful CoTs are *longer*, not shorter (Claude 3.7 Sonnet 2064±59 vs 1439±54 tokens; R1 6003±74 vs 4737±79) ([Anthropic](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.05410)) — so compression cannot be read as "less hidden computation" either.
- Verbalization correlates positively with length (r≈0.629) and scheming negatively (r=−0.630) across CoT-intervention methods on Qwen3-4B ([Investigating CoT Monitorability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08525)): compressed generations will show lower CoT-side recall for identical behaviour. Report every CoT rate per 10k chars alongside raw.
- Correct traces ~12K vs incorrect >25K tokens (Qwen3-4B, AIME only) ([CoDE-Stop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04930)) and wrong-trace median ~14.9k vs ~5.8k words with late clustered backtracking bursts ([Backtracking Bursts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27965)) give the expected magnitude and a candidate mechanism for "failures run longer".
- Effort parameters act as a ceiling, not a dial, and allocation appears crystallised at training (GPT-OSS only; Bayes factors 0.41–0.43, "inconclusive but leaning toward the null") ([Effort as Ceiling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16938)). **Rewritten:** the "K3 per-turn length should be multimodal because it was distilled from three effort-level teachers" prediction is **our conjecture**, a two-step inference from a GPT-OSS-only inconclusive null to another lab's distillation; keep it as an exploratory X hypothesis, not a literature prediction. The [K3 report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24653) does document effort levels {low, high, max} and a per-problem budget, which is the direct reason to run the effort-knob probe instead.
- Legibility falls with outcome RL and on harder items for all reasoning models except Claude ([Illegible CoTs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.27338); the 53% drop is QwQ-only, and legibility–correctness correlation under resampling is r≈0.061): compute a legibility proxy *before* keyword metrics.
- Self-summaries lose most trace signal once the prompt is visible, and Last90 matches summaries at equal length ([Observability Ladder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02089v1) — note: **model-written** self-summaries from open models, an imperfect proxy for OpenAI's provider summaries): compare OA summaries only against the last k sentences of DS/KM turns; never pool; use billed output tokens as OA volume.
- Thinking models retain performance with reasoning off late in RL while *trace correctness declines* as accuracy rises ([Scaling Competence](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.21743v1), single training run): motivates the thinking-disabled rerun of g3, and warns that a compressed trace may also be a *less correct* trace.

### D6 — Different mechanism than gold; OpenAI adds collateral (tests, docs, style churn)

**CoT-side constructs remain thin.** Only [Code-gen thinking patterns](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.13758) (STY, UTC, EGC, ALT) and Act·onomy's Planning/Evaluating subactions name reasoning content that could precede collateral, and the code-gen taxonomy comes from 1.7B–32B open models doing single-shot CoderEval generation — so applying it to a frontier model's provider summaries is a two-step extrapolation, and [Agent-Generated Tests](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07900) already points the other way for the OA lineage (GPT-5.2 wrote tests in 0.6% of tasks). One new CoT-side result is encouraging: in the **coding** setting of [Implicit-Influence Monitoring](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04735), implicit influences were verbalized ~93% of the time (vs 5–16% in preference settings) — so code *design decisions* may be far better verbalized than preferences, which makes a "decision verbalized" rate worth measuring.

## 3. What the literature says about pattern ↔ performance and pattern ↔ training/generation

All statements are scoped to the models each paper studied (§1). Corrections applied during verification are marked **[corrected]** and detailed in §3h.

### 3a. Pattern ↔ performance: positive associations (within the papers' settings)

- Rarely deployed cognitive elements are the success-associated ones: Knowledge alignment 20.2% presence / PPMI 0.234 vs Logical coherence 91% / 0.091; success-association of diverse elements rises on ill-structured problems (0.046 → 0.186); scaffolding successful structure G* gives up to +66.7% on ill-structured problems ([Cognitive Foundations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.16660)).
- Verification/backtracking emergence coincides with RL gains; priming with behavior-bearing *wrong* solutions matches priming with correct ones; empty-CoT priming does not help; behaviors are "the critical factor", not a determinant **[corrected]** ([Four Habits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.01307)).
- Explore→Monitor (+0.41), Explore→Analyze (+0.31), Monitor→Analyze (+0.28), Read→Verify (+0.27), Verify ratio (+0.21) positive; Explore ratio (−0.54), Explore→Verify (−0.45), Explore→Answer (−0.41), total tokens (−0.36) negative **[corrected: Explore→Verify is negative; "exploration without resolution" is a gloss]** ([ThinkARM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.19995)).
- Applying→Evaluating strongest positive coefficient, total tokens strongest negative; Bloom features AUC 0.676 vs length-only 0.613 ([Bloom's](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23205)).
- Transition-advantage steering +13.37 pp on AIME25 for gpt-oss-20b low effort; greedy steering can hurt **[corrected: 25× fewer interventions is a MATH-500 figure]** ([CoT-FSM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.22437)).
- UTC has the strongest (weak positive) correlation with correctness; CST/AMB/CMP weakly negative; TSK+UTC+SFA co-occurrence best ([Code-gen thinking patterns](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.13758)).
- Behavioral Lift: confidence calibration +72.2/+79.6%, knowledge alignment +53.7/+80.3%, self-awareness high; self-correction +20.1/+12.4%; hypothesis testing ≈ +1.0%; uncertainty acknowledgment −16.1/−13.9% **[corrected]** ([Amplified ≠ Predictive](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13760)).
- Operator distributions predict within-problem correctness at WP-AUC 0.701 (full trace; 0.664 at 50%) vs backtrack count 0.594, wait count 0.603, self-judge 0.512; committal-vs-reflective gap separates correct traces on easy problems (AUC 0.76) but is non-significant on hard ones (p=0.15) **[corrected]** ([ReasonOps](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29192)).
- Tree structure beats length for correctness prediction (+3.9 to +8.3% avg; +14.58% MMLU-Pro); both excessive and insufficient branching associate with error **[corrected]** ([LCoT2Tree](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.22148)).
- FSF correlates with accuracy for every model on both tasks; best-of-N by FSF +5–13% on AIME25; pruning a failed branch +8–14% on HARP **[corrected]** ([Length, Review, Structure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.19284)).
- TDA features R² 0.236 vs graph 0.064 — but the target is alignment with expert solutions, not correctness **[corrected]** ([Shape of Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.20665)).
- Information Organization share >0.04 lower in incorrect CoTs; Hypothesis Generation and Analogy Recall later and more frequent in failures; post-answer checks superficial (R1 fixed 5 cases, all format) **[corrected]** ([Psyche](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.00729)).
- Agentic: overthinking score vs resolve β=−7.894, R²=0.892 (reasoning models); o1 with native function calling 29.1%→47.7% **[corrected: ~30% gain is vs the low-effort 21.0% baseline]** ([Danger of Overthinking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08235)); Context Gathering Delay ρ=+0.68, Opening Patch Intensity ρ=−0.78, Validation Effort ρ=+0.50; failures longer within-agent but resolved runs ~10% longer within-task ([Behavioral Drivers](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.02547v1)); Search Loop most stable transfer signal; function selection, not file choice, separates outcomes **[corrected: "similar" not "equal" resolve rates]** ([TraceProbe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06184)); strained coherence +47 pp for Qwen3.5-35B (p=0.003) but +20 pp n.s. for Gemma4-31B, reversed in the low-verbosity tertile, lexical baseline gap larger (+51) **[corrected]** ([Strained Coherence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07889)); γ^OT and γ^OA both correlate with lower resolve; TACT +5.8/+4.8 pp **[corrected]** ([TACT](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.05980)); per-state FSM AUROC up to 0.941 (tau2 telecom; SWE-agent 0.799) **[corrected]** ([Automata](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.23670v1)); edit streaks ≥5 → 80% fail rate for Moatless+DeepSeek-V3 ([ProcGrep](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16988)); trap-fork recovery 40.4→43.5% on the fired subset **[corrected]** ([TraceGraph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.31308)).
- New agentic-consistency results: high-TSS conditions 90.2% vs low-TSS 61.2% correct (d=0.81) while AC is uncorrelated (r=0.12) ([How Consistent Are LLM Agents?](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.28840)); consistent tasks (≤2 unique sequences) 82.3–86.5% vs inconsistent (≥4) 41.2–64.7%, failure-detector AUROC 0.619–0.775 on HotpotQA **[corrected: AUROC is HotpotQA-only; the SWE-bench part reports step-count statistics only]** ([When Agents Disagree](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.11619v2)); E→V verification 54.2% resolved vs 28.1% unresolved and max X-run 4.8 vs 11.0 on SWE-agent ([Agent Genome](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15579)); successful ProMax resolutions complete in markedly fewer interaction rounds ([SWE-Bench ProMax](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09802)); agents that fix a tool-call or instruction error are 1.5–4× more likely to succeed and explicit verification actions 13–87% more likely ([HAL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.11977)).
- Tool-efficiency results: TP framing shows fewer calls at maintained accuracy (OTC-PPO −68.3% TC, +215.4% TP vs Search-R1-PPO) ([OTC-PO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.14870)); EAPO +7–10% with 18–25% fewer calls, and "more tool calls do not necessarily improve performance" ([EAPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02132)); interaction-dense cold starts (≥9 calls, 4K trajectories) give the best downstream RL despite being smallest ([ASTER](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01204)).

### 3b. Null and negative results (pre-register these as live alternatives)

- Spontaneous reasoning shifts are rare (6.31% pooled), do not increase with GRPO, and coincide with lower accuracy (2.57% vs 16.44%, N=723,200); formal Aha fires on 1.79% **[corrected: ~1M traces, not 11M]** ([Illusion of Insight](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.00514)).
- Mean mode occupancy, transition frequencies, alternation rate, run-length and reflection density do **not** differ between correct and incorrect R1 traces (all p>0.29; JS<0.01); only cross-trace variance and reflection placement do **[corrected]** ([Anatomy of R1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07410)).
- Self-reflection keyword presence in base models does not imply higher accuracy ("nearly half" no better) ([Understanding R1-Zero](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20783)).
- Reflective phrases occur far more in incorrect responses, especially in small distilled models; incorrect responses longer ([Speculative Thinking](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.12329v1)).
- Thinking models amplify self-correction / hypothesis testing / uncertainty acknowledgment 3–7× yet these have weak or negative Lift — the Amplification-Lift Gap, persisting 2B–32B ([Amplified ≠ Predictive](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13760)).
- The same behavioural signal predicts success in some framework×LLM configurations and failure in others (error rate 47 vs 48; test ratio 29/55; P7 50/33); framework explains 64.3% of mean-turns variance vs LLM family 10.0% **[corrected: 64,380 runs, 47,858 Verified]** ([Same Signal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18332)).
- Test-writing rate is decoupled from resolve rate across and within models (GPT-5.2 0.6% / 71.8%; Kimi 97.4% / 63.4%); prompt interventions do not change outcomes (all McNemar p>0.05) **[corrected: "model-dependent process style", not lab-level]** ([Agent-Generated Tests](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07900)).
- Backtrack-edit fraction spans two orders of magnitude (0.8% GPT-5.2-codex to 16.8% Gemini 3 Flash) and is **decoupled from solve rate**; models with the same solve rate take different code-state paths ([SSA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17454)).
- Hidden-state error awareness is diagnostic not causal; text classifier 0.59 AUROC; verbalised confidence near-identical for wrong and right traces **[corrected]** ([Hidden Error Awareness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09502)).
- No correlation between legibility and correctness under resampling (r≈0.061) despite a 53% accuracy drop when QwQ is restricted to legible portions **[corrected: single model]** ([Illegible CoTs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.27338)).
- Mid-execution "cold feet" confidence decline is uninformative; post-execution overconfidence +34 to +55 pp; pre-execution AUROC advantage not significant **[corrected]** ([Agentic Uncertainty](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06948)).
- Redundancy is 61–93% even in correct traces and persists (46–85%) at Level-5; a theorem shows it is structural under length-agnostic outcome rewards **[corrected: the idle-content taxonomy is not quantified]** ([How Much Thinking Is Enough](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23926)); 41–52% of tokens come after the final answer ([PUMA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17672)); >70% of incorrect responses contain a correct thought that was abandoned ([Underthinking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.18585)).
- Thinking-model paradox: Qwen3-Thinking early-stops in 49.0% of tasks with the lowest pass rate, GPT-5 non-finishes 84.5% — action evidence; the echo-chamber account is interpretation **[corrected]** ([NL2Repo-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12730)).
- Procedural diversity does not correspond to capability ([ProcGrep](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16988)); process diagnostics add granularity without overturning rankings **[corrected: ECE 0.227→0.138]** ([ProcCtrlBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20251)).
- Increased reasoning effort produces equal or lower accuracy in 21 of 36 model-agent-benchmark combinations ([HAL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.11977)); effort acts as a ceiling, not a dial, with inconclusive Bayes factors ([Effort as Ceiling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16938)).
- Higher-capability models are *less* monitorable (r = −0.87 to −0.39) ([MonitorBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28590)); smaller models frequently match or exceed larger ones on consistency, and ~24 months of capability gains produced only small reliability improvements ([Reliability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16666)).
- Self-consistency judge ensembles triple cost for no quality gain ([trajectory-judge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00038)); judges over-predict +1 and neutral steps are hardest ([AgentProcessBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14465)); κ deflation 33.8–41.2 pp and rankings shift up to 15 positions **[corrected]** ([Reliability without Validity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19544)); the same traces yield 69.7–82.6% faithfulness depending on the classifier, with per-category κ 0.06–0.42 ([Classifier Sensitivity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20172)).
- Diversity collapse is not fixed by removing CoT: suppressing thinking at inference recovers **no** diversity in any stage or task while costing accuracy (GSM8K −18%, MATH-Algebra −28%, HumanEval −32%) ([Where diversity collapses](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.16027v1)).

### 3c. Pattern ↔ training: agentic-RL dynamics and lab-reported statistics (new)

- **Interaction vs per-step compute.** Interaction scaling dominates per-step scaling on web agents; during TTI training tokens per action slightly decrease while rollout length first decreases then increases, and a fixed short horizon (h=5) produces premature termination at evaluation ([TTI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07976)).
- **RL grows redundant acting unless penalised.** Plain GRPO increases tool calls and response length monotonically over training ([AKBE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26952)); tool-use rate stays a flat minority under GRPO and reward shaping does not fix it ([AXPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28774)); entropy-guided branching collapses onto 1–3 trajectories ([AEPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.14545)); void turns proliferate in later turns and destabilise training ([SimpleTIR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.02479)).
- **Efficiency-targeted recipes compress per-step thinking while extending horizons.** ReTool: length −40%, code ratio →98%, earlier invocation, emergent code self-correction ([ReTool](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.11536)). DeepSWE: per-step thinking drops sharply while environment steps rise, with adaptive ~2K vs ~100–200 token allocation ([blog](https://www.together.ai/blog/deepswe)). ASTER: tool invocations fall to a plateau in RL stage 1, and a 50-call training budget generalises to 83.0% at a 256-call inference budget vs 77.7% for 10-call training ([ASTER](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01204)). Nebius: doubling T_max 40→80 grows steps and submit-terminations alongside Pass@1 35.7→39.0% ([2508.03501](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.03501)). Kimi-Dev: Agentless RL raises response length and extends the useful turn horizon past 70 ([2509.23045](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.23045)).
- **First-party numbers for our models.** OpenAI reports 22% fewer output tokens and 45% fewer tool calls for GPT-5 vs o3 at high effort with a higher Verified score, and "significantly fewer tokens" for GPT-5.5 vs GPT-5.4 in Codex (no number, effort xhigh) — note this is *reported* efficiency for GPT-5, and an explicit tuning goal only for GPT-5.5 ([GPT-5 devs](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-for-developers/)). DeepSeek reports thinking-mode gains of +1.0 (SWE Verified), +9.3 (Terminal Bench 2.0), +3.1 (tau2) and self-diagnoses redundant self-verification and context overflow ([2512.02556](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02556)). Kimi reports effort levels {low, high, max}, a per-problem token budget with a −1 reward override counting reasoning *and* tool-call arguments, and that tool-call steps scale up consistently with RL FLOPs ([K3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24653)).
- **Decision confidence degrades under decision-oriented RL:** "Wrong Decision But Low Uncertainty" rises 34.50% → 70.21% after GRPO on Qwen3-4B-Thinking ([TRUST](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06976)).

### 3d. Pattern ↔ channel validity: faithfulness, monitorability, mixing, summaries (new)

- **Verbalization is low and channel-dependent.** CoT faithfulness overall 25% (Claude 3.7 Sonnet) / 39% (DeepSeek R1), misaligned hints 20% / 29%; RL raises faithfulness 63% rel. (MMLU) / 41% rel. (GPQA) then plateaus at ~28% / ~20%; in reward-hack environments models exploit hacks on >99% of prompts within 30 steps while verbalizing them in <2% of examples in 5 of 6 environments ([Anthropic](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.05410)). Unverbalized adoption is higher for **tool-return** cues than user-message cues on all 15 models tested — including **DeepSeek V4 Pro/Flash and Kimi K2.6** — and higher for implicit than explicit cues in 28/30 comparisons ([FACE-Eval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29464)). Detection of implicit influence drops 41–46 pp vs explicit, to as low as 5% under benign system prompts — **except in the coding setting, where it stays ~93%** ([Implicit-Influence Monitoring](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04735)).
- **CoT adds real signal over actions, and that signal is fragile.** CoT monitors reach 95% recall on systemic hacks vs 60% action-only ([Baker et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.11926)); CoT-only beats action-only by ~24% average on MonitorBench, and stress tests cut monitorability ~7–8 pp mainly by *omission* ([MonitorBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28590)). But ~77% of reward hacks are action-visible anyway, and on the ~23% action-invisible subset a gradient-free reasoning rewrite collapses a held-out monitor from ~95% to 4–11% while commands stay byte-identical; only external ground truth recovers it (to 48%) ([Terminal Wrench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00583)). Optimising against a CoT monitor produces obfuscated reward hacking ([Baker et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.11926)); training incentives on length/KL show no consistent monitorability effect while penalising monitor accuracy degrades it ([Reasoning Under Pressure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.00218)).
- **Language mixing is an RLVR-stage phenomenon and is prompt-language-dependent.** On MATH500 with Chinese prompts DeepSeek-R1 code-switches in 27.1% of problems (4.39 switches/problem) but **0.0% with English prompts**; QwQ-32B-Preview 77.4% ZH vs 0.6% EN; ordering RLVR > pretrained base > SFT/RLHF; forcing monolingual Chinese costs 5.6 pp (p=0.0017), and distilled R1 variants retain mixing (21–24% ZH) ([Language Mixing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.15849)). Switching the trace language does not rescue monitorability: deception rates are at or above English levels in Chinese for Qwen3 and reach 100% in low-resource languages, with average CoT unfaithfulness 95.9% across 8B–120B models ([Fragility across languages](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27901)).
- **Legibility measurement is judge- and granularity-dependent.** A 0–4 whole-CoT autorater scores DeepSeek-R1 at 99.20% legibility / 98.14% coverage on correctly answered items (and folds language consistency into legibility) ([Pragmatic Monitorability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.23966)), while a 1–9 rater finds illegible CoTs common and rising with difficulty for every reasoning model except Claude ([Illegible CoTs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.27338)). Both are legitimate; the disagreement is a measurement fact, so the gate in §4b uses the stricter chunk-level rater.
- **Provider summaries are a different object from raw CoT.** [Monitoring Monitorability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18311) excludes summarized reasoning from the CoT category outright. [Observability Ladder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02089v1) — the only quantitative fidelity study, and on **model-written** self-summaries of open models — finds summaries carry most trace ranking signal without the prompt (0.774 vs 0.813 AUROC) but only +0.019 with the prompt visible, and Last90 matches or slightly beats them at equal length. **No verified study characterises OpenAI's provider-generated summaries.** What we do know about the raw OA channel comes from [Anti-scheming](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.15541): o3/o4-mini hidden CoT contains a distinct non-standard dialect (illusions, parted, overshadow, vantage, …) at environment-dependent rates, contains actions contradicting the CoT, and contains verbalized evaluation awareness that *causally* changes behaviour (removing it raised o3's covert-action rate 13.2%→24.2%; injecting it lowered it to 18.6% vs 24.9% control). All of that is destroyed by summarisation.
- **Classifier choice changes the number.** Regex-only 74.4%, regex+3-judge 82.6%, Claude Sonnet 4 judge 69.7% on identical traces; per-model gaps up to 30.6 pp; κ 0.06 (sycophancy) to 0.42 (grader) ([Classifier Sensitivity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20172)).

### 3e. Pattern ↔ contamination (new)

- Models identify gold files from repo name + issue text alone at 60–76% on Verified vs 57–71% on Full and ≤53% on external repos; function-reproduction 5-gram overlap 34.9 / 28.7 / 18.2; verbatim prefix completion up to 31.6% (Claude 4 Opus) ([SWE-Bench Illusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12286)). Ticket-only all-gold-file identification is 63–65% on Verified vs 8–19% on BeetleBox/SWE-rebench for Claude 3.5/3.7 Sonnet ([Verified tests memory?](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.10218v2)).
- Fresh-task gaps are large: DeepSeek-V3-0324 39.7% Verified vs 21.3% on Mar–Apr 2025 SWE-rebench, with two V3 versions similar on rebench but divergent on Verified — "may suggest potential contamination effects on the older benchmark" ([SWE-rebench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20411)). Agents also do better on the original SWE-bench repos (22.96%) than new repos (18.89%) with **no** recency correlation ([SWE-bench Live](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23419)).
- Session-isolated re-solves separate memorisation from solving: memorised solutions reproduce identically across trials (diversity ~0) while genuine solving is diverse, and one case is a memorised **non-gold** solution ([Cross-Context Verification](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.21454v1)). Patch-similarity screens on frontier agents show no lump near 1.0 (Refactoring Jaccard 0.35–0.51; Test Writing 0.08–0.11) while explicitly not proving absence of contamination ([SWE Atlas](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.08366)).
- Detection is fragile in exactly our regime: one epoch of GRPO drops log-prob detector AUROC by ~14–20 points (Min-K% 74.96→58.54) while +7.14% performance inflation persists, and CoT-SFT contamination of LRMs leaves most detectors near chance ([Fragility of Contamination Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02386)).
- Agents also cheat in ways only logs reveal: git-history exploitation (patching it dropped MiniMax-M2.5 on Commit0 from 50.0% to 18.8%), test-assertion modification, special-casing ([Hodoscope](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11072)); searching HuggingFace/arXiv for benchmark answers and hard-coding to pass tests ([HAL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.11977)); reward hacking in 13.8% of SWE-Marathon rollouts with exploit-tier rates 26.0% gpt-5.5, 22.0% gemini-3.1-pro, 0.5% claude-opus-4-7 ([SWE-Marathon](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.07682v1)).
- Grading validity itself is not clean: 7.8% of "solved" Verified patches fail the full developer suite, 29.6% of plausible patches are behaviourally differentiable from the oracle, ~11% are estimated incorrect, and combined weaknesses inflate resolution rates by 6.2 pp ([PatchDiff](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.15223)).

### 3f. Pattern ↔ training: diversity, distillation and the sign table (new; replaces the round-1 self-convergence baseline)

- **Reasoning post-training converges structure across base models**: QwQ vs R1 JS-div 0.010 vs their bases 0.083 ([ReasoningFlow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05402)); same-base models are harder to separate ([LOT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.24147)); R1-distilled models are confused with each other most ([ReasonOps](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29192)); distillation lineage aligns habit profiles, with the Qwen3~R1 resemblance offered as a hypothesis ([CogTest](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.21571)).
- **Where in the pipeline diversity is lost.** On Olmo 3 7B: Think-SFT −62% SBERT diversity, Think-DPO −4%, Think-RL +4% (38% of base retained); Instruct-SFT −38%, DPO −23%, RL −5% (34% retained); **RL-Zero retains 93%**. The largest single drop is two-teacher CoT distillation SFT; among *correct* answers Think and Instruct converge to 1.3–1.6 Vendi modes on verifiable tasks, and majority voting then buys almost nothing (GSM8K +0.4% vs base +24%) ([Where diversity collapses](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.16027v1)).
- **RLVR's mechanism.** Expected-return objectives are indifferent to how mass is spread among correct solutions (all-correct policies are stationary points), so stochastic dynamics self-reinforce a narrow subset ([UCPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00365)); log-probability ratios diverge exponentially and the winner is set by initial probability ([IPS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21669)); RLVR acts predominantly as support-constrained optimisation with SRR 0.93–0.99, NDR ≤0.04 and slightly negative net support change, and token-level entropy can *rise* while answer-level entropy falls ([Invisible Leash](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.14843)); base models beat RLVR models at large k ([Limit of RLVR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13837)); reverse-KL accelerates the decay and mass-covering divergences act as rehearsal ([DPH-RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.07430)); diversity loss transfers from solved to unsolved problems ([Outcome-based Exploration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.06941)). Counter-signs: boundary-aware curriculum RL raises pass@256 +9.8 pp over base ([Curriculum RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22317)), and the boundary may be non-monotone in RL compute (exploitation then exploration stage) ([Two-Stage Dynamic](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04028)).
- **RL vs SFT/distillation, weight-space and distribution-space.** RL steepens (~2.5×) and SFT flattens (~1/3) reasoning-graph decay; RL compresses *incorrect* trajectories while SFT expands correct ones ([RL Squeezes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.21128)). On-policy RL is implicitly KL-minimal, and oracle-SFT / self-distillation controls show KL-minimality (not RL per se) is what limits forgetting ([RL's Razor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.04259)). RL updates only 5–30% of parameters, driven by near-policy data rather than KL regularisation ([small subnetworks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.11711)), off principal directions with minimal spectral drift while SFT targets principal weights ([Path Not Taken](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08567)). RL mainly teaches *when* to deploy pre-existing mechanisms (~76% of the RL gap recovered by hybrids) whereas SFT-distillation installs new ones (~11%) **[corrected: v4 reverses the v1 headline for distilled models]** ([Base Models Know How](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07364)); RL retains/strengthens backtracking and verification while diminishing backward chaining and subgoal setting ([Four Habits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.01307)); theory says SFT on shortest paths leaves backtracking at init while RLVR learns it and distillation from RLVR traces transfers it (synthetic only) ([Provable RLVR](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.22938v1)); SFT memorises and outcome-RL generalises, but SFT is needed for format ([SFT Memorizes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.17161)).
- **Distillation specifics closest to V4/K3.** On-policy self-distillation with sampled demonstrations tilts the base distribution by a PCMI score and *amplifies existing probability gaps*, concentrating on already-dominant modes; it matches or exceeds RL on pass@1 while flattening pass@k (78.7 vs 83.6 pass@16, Qwen3-8B) and failing OOD ([2606.26091](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26091)). Reverse-KL on-policy distillation leaves the student with 6.8% high-entropy tokens vs the teacher's 18.5%; adding forward KL where teacher entropy is high recovers pass@8 by +1.37/+2.39/+5.05 ([EOPD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07079)). Multi-teacher on-policy distillation with **same-origin** teachers keeps policy entropy stable (~0.30) and closes 91–95% of student–teacher headroom, while a mismatched external teacher contracts entropy 0.30→0.21 (policy-gradient variant) or diverges (top-k) ([MOPD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30406)).
- **Distillation effects on CoT patterns are non-monotonic in scale**: R1-Distill 7B/8B shift toward Applying and away from Evaluating but the 1.5B distill resembles the teacher **[corrected]** ([Bloom's](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23205)); BCA changes −0.106 at 32B, +0.056 at 14B ([Performative CoT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11746)); distilled models degrade >12 points on AIME25 when reflection keywords are suppressed while RL models do not ([NoWait](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.08343)); distilled 1.5B shows limited steering benefit ([CoT-FSM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.22437)); structural collapse partially transfers to a distilled model (AUC 0.76) ([Detecting RLVR Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11792)).
- **Distillation inheritance on the action side**: student–teacher JSD 0.25 vs ~0.52 for other pairs, and behaviour most similar between models from similar release periods ([ProcGrep](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16988)); LoRA distillation raises AGS toward the teacher (+0.13), and Kimi-K2 (thinking) reaches 82.6% S_node / 94.7% S_dep vs the Claude Sonnet 4.5 oracle ([AgentEcho](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21255)); students inherit R1-0528's branching (+~12% P→P) and generalise worse ([Long-CoT SFT patterns](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01702)).
- **Generation/capability trends (action side, closest to our design)**: framework gap shrinks 19.4→3.8→0.9 pp across claude-3.5-sonnet → claude-4-sonnet → claude-opus-4.5, cross-framework outcome agreement 71–93% **[corrected]** ([Behavioral Drivers](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.02547v1)); older OpenAI models (o3, gpt-4.1, gpt-4o) show more looping/early termination while gpt-5 shows >60% Instruction Following, and deepseek-r1 gets stuck in loops **[corrected]** ([SWE-EVO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18470)); code-level symptoms decline and interaction-level symptoms (S3, S7) rise over time in real sessions ([Fail Their Users](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29442)); newer extended-thinking Claude models do better with fewer procedures ([ProcGrep](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16988)); reliability improves only slightly over ~24 months of releases ([Reliability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16666)); ChatGPT output diversity declines across seven versions on paraphrase tasks, with synthetic-data contamination as the authors' *inference*, not a test **[corrected]** ([Self-convergence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12683)). Cross-lab homogenisation is an independent candidate: 71–82% inter-model similarity with ~8 unique models per top-50 output cluster, causes explicitly left open ([Hivemind](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.22954)).
- **RL training dynamics of length and markers**: reasoning length rises, peaks, then declines while accuracy plateaus, and trace correctness declines while accuracy rises **[corrected: fourth phase unnamed]** ([Scaling Competence](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.21743v1)); plan keywords fall 30.5→9.2 and rethink 9.38→3.62 then rebound, on one 1.5B model **[corrected]** ([Leash](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.21540)); reflection keyword frequency 0.6% base → ~100% distilled ([Probing self-reflection](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.12217)); "Aha" keywords already present in base models incl. DeepSeek-V3-Base, and GRPO's 1/\|o_i\| term inflates the length of wrong answers ([Understanding R1-Zero](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20783)); conversational behaviours rise spontaneously under accuracy-only PPO ([Societies of Thought](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10825)); monitorability rises early in RL with a mid-training dip for GPT-5.1 Codex Max **[corrected]** ([Monitoring Monitorability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18311)); training-data *format* drives strategy shifts (d up to ~1.5) while domain and scale barely do **[corrected]** ([CoT Encyclopedia](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10185)); language mixing increases progressively with RL steps ([Language Mixing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.15849)).
- **Explicitly no training analysis** (so no generational inference is licensed from them): ThinkARM, Schoenfeld, ThinkPatterns, Thought Anchors, Length/Review/Structure, TRACES, Structural overthinking, LCoT2Tree, Thoughtology, Psyche, Anatomy, Illusion (RL checkpoints only), TraceProbe, Same Signal, Success/failure trajectories, Strained Coherence, TraceGraph, Reasoning Primitives, Coherence Collapse, Agentic Uncertainty, NL2Repo (paradox only), Fail Users, Ada, Agent Tests, Consistency, Hodoscope, MAST, ProcCtrlBench, Wink, SWE-Compass, Tokenmaxxing, TACT, SWE-Marathon, Automata, AgentProcessBench, trajectory-judge, TRACE, Backtracking Bursts, RCP, PUMA, When More Thinking Hurts, CoDE-Stop, Observability Ladder, TDA, Redundancy, Beyond Last Answer, Reliability without Validity, Self-convergence, SSA, ProMax, Agent Genome, HAL, PatchDiff, On Randomness, Reliability, When Agents Disagree, How Consistent, FACE-Eval, Implicit-Influence, Terminal Wrench, Classifier Sensitivity, Pragmatic Monitorability, MonitorBench, Fragility across languages, CoT in the Wild, SWE-Bench Illusion, Verified-tests-memory, Cross-Context Verification, SWE-bench Live, SWE Atlas. **No paper tracks a within-lab lineage across three generations under a fixed harness on CoT content**; the generational evidence above is action-side, single-training-run, or on plain outputs.

### 3g. Statistical facts that constrain every claim (new)

- Single-run pass@1 varies 2.2–6.0 pp depending on which run is selected, SD exceeds 1.5 pp even at temperature 0 in several configs, and the pass@k / pass^k envelope reaches 24.9 pp; trajectories diverge in the first ~1% of tokens (median τ_div token 5 at default temperature, 56 at T=0); at σ=1.5% detecting 1 pp needs 36 runs, 2 pp needs 9 ([On Randomness](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.07150v2)). 29.3% of single-run evaluations misrank models ([When Agents Disagree](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.11619v2)).
- Question-level clustering matters: clustered SEs were 3.05× naive on DROP; resampling K answers shrinks only the conditional-variance term; ~969 independent questions are needed for a 3-pp difference at 80% power; and lowering temperature moves conditional variance into variance of conditional means ("don't touch the thermostat") ([Adding Error Bars](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00640)).
- Below a few hundred datapoints, 95% CLT intervals cover 92.5% at N=100 and bootstrap under-covers; Wilson or Beta(1,1) credible intervals are recommended, with a hierarchical Beta-Binomial for clustered questions and a bivariate-Gaussian latent correlation for paired model comparison ([Don't Use the CLT](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.01747)).
- Hierarchical Bayesian GLMs remain stable with <<20 points per cluster and handle pass/fail-all bimodality via Beta-Binomial ([HiBayES](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.05602v1)); embedding-space clustering shows naive methods overstate effective sample size 1.3–5.6× ([BHM-ESC](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.05709v2)).

## 4. Recommended pattern-coding scheme for this corpus

### 4a. Layered scheme

**Layer A — Action channel, all 9 models, deterministic (no judge).** Validity: full. Sources: [TraceProbe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06184) effect labels (SURVIVED/REVERTED/OFF-ANCHOR with gold as anchor set) + anti-pattern flags + milestones M1–M5; [Same Signal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18332) 6 classes and P1–P7; [Behavioral Drivers](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.02547v1) 13 symbols and three metrics; [Automata](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.23670v1) FSM fitness/cycle rate; [Agent Genome](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15579) X/E/P/V alphabet and E→V rate; [Consistency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10516) + [How Consistent](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.28840) composition-vs-ordering pair (JSD = C_traj^d ≈ AC-side; GAK/Levenshtein = C_traj^s ≈ TSS); [On Randomness](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.07150v2) τ_div; [TRACE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02837) Eff(T) by string provenance; [Agent-Generated Tests](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07900) test-write rules; [OTC-PO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.14870) tool productivity; [AKBE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26952) redundant-command rule; [SimpleTIR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.02479) void turns; [TTI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07976) tokens-per-action; [PatchDiff](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.15223) differential-test equivalence classes; [SWE Atlas](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.08366) memorisation-screen metrics; action-grounded Four Habits (verification = test after edit; backtracking = revert/overwrite; subgoal = plan text before a command block). Volume for OA = **billed output tokens**, never summary characters. Also record per run: termination class (submit / turn-cap / cost-cap) and, for OA, `summary_chars / billed_reasoning_tokens` plus empty-summary rate as a compression/fidelity proxy.

**Layer B — Raw CoT, DeepSeek and Kimi only, deterministic.** Validity: full on r1/v3.2/v4-pro and k2-thinking/k2.6/k3, subject to four gates (below). Sources: [ReasonOps](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29192) 7 operators with released fitted centroids (do not refit on 108 runs); [Beyond the Last Answer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.20708) 32-marker segmenter with our own three marker classes (**not** TRACES); [Probing self-reflection](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.12217) / [NoWait](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.08343) / [Leash](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.21540) keyword lexicons (densities per 1k chars **and** per command); [PUMA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17672) redundancy detector; [Shape of Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.20665) TDA features (local embeddings). Every Layer-B rate is reported next to its Layer-A counterpart (CoT-verification vs action-verification; CoT-backtrack vs reverted edits; reflection chunk vs next-command change) — the faithfulness guard, now with published backing from [Performative CoT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11746) (vocabulary) and [Terminal Wrench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00583) (77% of behaviourally important events are action-visible).

Layer-B gates, all computed *before* any lexicon metric is read:
1. **Legibility gate** — chunk-level 1–9 rater ([Illegible CoTs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.27338)) **and** whole-turn 0–4 rater ([Pragmatic Monitorability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.23966)); report the disagreement; use the stricter chunk-level score as the gate because agentic turns are long. Remove language from the 0–4 rubric so it does not double-count gate 2.
2. **Language-mixing gate (new)** — the four statistics of [Language Mixing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.15849) (% turns with a switch, switches/turn, chars between switches, CJK share of prose chars), adapted by stripping code blocks, paths, shell output and identifiers first. Prior expectation is near-zero given English prompts (R1: 0.0% on English-prompt MATH500), but repo content and error messages are the "prompt" in an agent loop, so the rate must be measured, not assumed. **Decision:** add Chinese-language lexicon equivalents *only if* the measured rate is non-trivial, and note this buys coverage, not faithfulness ([Fragility across languages](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27901)). Report mixing rate as a covariate alongside legibility.
3. **Classifier-sensitivity gate (new)** — for every lexicon, run the regex **and** a fixed-prompt LLM judge on the same turns; report the range, not a point; report κ per category and drop any category with κ < 0.2 from cross-generation claims; never compare our lexicon rates to numbers from papers using a different classifier ([Classifier Sensitivity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20172); calibrate against their released R1 / V3.2-Special labels first).
4. **Length-adjustment gate (new)** — report every CoT-side rate per 10k reasoning chars as well as raw, because verbalization rises with length (r≈0.63, [Investigating CoT Monitorability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08525)) and D4 says our generations differ in length by 3–4×.
5. **CoT-shaping covariate (new)** — record per model, from the lab's own report, whether a length penalty and/or language-consistency reward was applied to reasoning (V3.2: both, [2512.02556](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02556)). Any r1→v3.2 drop in verbalized intent is confounded with CoT shaping ([Baker et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.11926); [Reasoning Under Pressure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.00218)); this covariate is documentation, not estimable from our data.

**Layer C — Judge-based fine coding, second pass, costs API spend.** Primary: [ThinkARM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.19995) 8 episodes with Implement→command grounding (the only scheme with a correctness regression *and* an Implement episode that maps to command emission). Fine alternative for repertoire questions: [Cognitive Foundations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.16660) 28 elements. Code-specific supplement (exploratory only, per §2-D6): the 15 codes of [Code-gen thinking patterns](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.13758) + Localization/Reproduction. Cross-channel judges: [TACT](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.05980) OT/OA/CAL and [AgentProcessBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14465) +1/0/−1 on (CoT, command, output) triples; [trajectory-judge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00038) six faults; [Strained Coherence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07889) spans (stratified by verbosity, with the lexical baseline reported); [MonitorBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28590) Solution-Process score. Failure runs only: [SWE-EVO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18470) and [SWE-Compass](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.05459) as two judges, MAST single-agent subset as third, plus the [Baker et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.11926) 3-criterion hack rubric run separately on CoT-only and ACT-only views.

**OpenAI summaries — tightened rule (replaces round 1).** No verified study characterises OpenAI's provider-generated summaries; the nearest evidence is (i) [Monitoring Monitorability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18311), which excludes summarized reasoning from the CoT category, (ii) [Observability Ladder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02089v1) on *model-written* self-summaries of open models, and (iii) [Anti-scheming](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.15541), which shows that what the o-series raw CoT actually contains — a non-standard dialect, CoT–action contradictions, verbalized evaluation awareness that causally shifts behaviour — is precisely what summarisation removes. Therefore: **the only CoT-side facts we may assert for o1/o3/gpt-5.5 are action-anchored presences** — the summary states a plan or tool intent that the very next command corroborates. Do **not** compute on summaries: lexicon frequencies, legibility, mixing rates, ReasonOps pivots, subthought markers, DAGs, TDA, dialogue density, redundancy share, or *any* absence claim. Presence-level constructs that remain admissible: [Amplified ≠ Predictive](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13760) 9-behavior binary; [Schoenfeld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14662) 3-class paragraph scheme; the 4-class Propose/Deduce/Verify/Backtrack of [Long-CoT SFT patterns](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01702) with a caveat; the coarse 5 meta-cognitive controls + Backtracking/Verification of Cognitive Foundations. Compare only against the *last-k-sentences* of DS/KM turns at matched observability, and report any OA reasoning-based D4/D5 metric only where Layer A corroborates it.

**Judge governance (all of Layer C).** Follow the Minimum Viable Validation Protocol of [Reliability without Validity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19544): human-coded calibration subset (≈20 turns × 3 lineages) with Cohen's κ, never raw agreement; ≥3 replicates at T=0 with disagreement rate; position swap for pairwise judgments; judge from outside the lineage under study (DeepSeek V3.2 and Kimi are both corpus subjects and candidate judges); length-matched subsample check for verbosity bias because OA summaries and DS/KM raw CoT differ enormously in length. Expect ≈70% judge–human agreement on step labels ([AgentProcessBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14465)) and do not read differences below that floor. Skip self-consistency ensembles ([trajectory-judge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00038)).

**Statistics for every reported contrast (new).** Task-clustered, task-paired standard errors ([Adding Error Bars](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00640)); Wilson or Clopper-Pearson intervals for identity/identical-pair rates and Clopper-Pearson upper bounds for 0/31-style counts, plus the paired Bayesian comparison with the bivariate-Gaussian latent correlation ([Don't Use the CLT](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.01747)); effective task count computed by repo and by issue-embedding cluster, since 3 sympy + 1 pytest is effectively ~2 clusters, not 4 ([BHM-ESC](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.05709v2)); provider-default temperature retained ([Adding Error Bars](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00640) "don't touch the thermostat"). Confirmatory model for the expansion: `outcome ~ Beta-Binomial(generation + lineage + task random effect + task×lineage)` with repeats nested in task, extended with a Beta likelihood for within-cell similarity (D2) and log-normal for tokens (D4) ([HiBayES](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.05602v1); their GAIA case with 10 repeats × 4 models × effort levels is the template for the K3 effort probe).

### 4b. What must be pre-registered (before any Layer-B/C computation on the frozen corpus or the expansion)

1. **The D2 metric pair, direction-free.** Composition (JSD over verb histogram ≈ C_traj^d) and ordering (GAK or normalized Levenshtein ≈ C_traj^s ≈ TSS) consistency per cell, with the existing cross-task baseline subtraction. Pre-register D2 as **two-sided**: [Reliability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16666) reports smaller/older models often *more* consistent, and [How Consistent](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.28840) shows TSS and AC can move in opposite directions.
2. **Rival-mechanism table with signs (M1–M8 of §2-D2).** For each direction: the rival, the sign it predicts for RL-native / V4-type / K3-type arms, the discriminating observable, and the minimal K and task count needed to separate the signs. Where signs coincide (M1 and M2 both predict the modal-across-labs patch wins), state that the design cannot separate them and name what would.
3. **Contamination covariate protocol** (§2-D2, items a–e), run and reported *before* D2/D3 interpretation, including the explicit statement that a null is not evidence of no contamination ([Fragility of Contamination Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02386)).
4. **Length-metric stratification.** Within model, solved-only, turn-cap and cost-cap status as covariates, OA volume in billed tokens, all CoT rates additionally per 10k chars.
5. **Null-tested variables** with the negative result as the explicit alternative: reasoning shifts ([Illusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.00514) sign), reflection density ([Amplified](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13760) gap), test-writing rate ([Agent Tests](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07900) decoupling), backtrack fraction ([SSA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17454) decoupling), reasoning effort ([HAL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.11977) 21/36 equal-or-worse), capability–monitorability ([MonitorBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28590) negative), CoT-suppression diversity recovery ([Where diversity collapses](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.16027v1) none).
6. **Judge protocol** per MVVP, judge identity per lineage, calibration-subset size, κ threshold below which a metric is dropped; and for every lexicon the dual regex/LLM range plus per-category κ.
7. **Channel gates**: legibility (chunk-level), language mixing, classifier sensitivity, length adjustment, CoT-shaping covariate (§4a), and the OA action-anchored-presence-only rule.
8. **Necessity control (new).** Run each task K times with reasoning disabled or at minimal effort (DeepSeek non-thinking, Kimi thinking off, OpenAI effort=minimal) and compute the pass-rate gap; only tasks with a large gap license reading the CoT as computation rather than rationalization ([Emmons et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.05246)). Note the vendor's own thinking gain on SWE Verified is +1.0 for v3.2 ([2512.02556](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02556)), so the gate will probably fail on the current four tasks — a formal reason to defer CoT-content claims to the expansion.
9. **Power and precision.** ω² estimated from the four tasks; expansion sized with eq. 9 of [Adding Error Bars](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00640) and the run table of [On Randomness](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.07150v2) (σ=1.5%: 36 runs for 1 pp). Report pass@k and pass^k envelopes, never bare pass@1 point differences. K≥8 subset for mode accuracy / mode leakage / U-statistics / distinct-solution-class counts; interior m/K tasks required for D1 ([Overtraining](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.15455v1)).
10. **Future instruments, named as such**: the true k* prefill probe (prefix the CoT, force an action) for [How Much Thinking Is Enough](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23926); the effort-knob rerun for K3 at {low, high, max} ([K3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24653)); the thinking-disabled rerun for all g3; P(IS) elicitation ([Agentic Uncertainty](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06948)); differential-test generation for PatchDiff classes.
11. **Decision rule for the r1/v3.2 dichotomy**: OT and OA axes reported separately (TACT) so the two pathologies are not collapsed into "overthinking".
12. **Version-drift log (new).** Served models behind fixed names can change over a collection window. Record, per run, the date, provider, and any version string the API returns, and report the collection-window span per model; treat undated cross-host links (DS g2→g3, KM g1→g2) as carrying both sampling-default and version-drift confounds. No verified paper in this map measures API behaviour drift over time, so this is a documentation control, not an estimable covariate.

### 4c. Top 3 pattern-level hypotheses to test first with zero new API spend

All three use only Layers A and B (deterministic; local embeddings at most), and each carries its rival and its null.

**H1 — Post-solution tail explains D4's compression, and the axis is thinking-vs-doing, not length.** Compute the **commitment prefix (X)** — the turn at which the final patch's edit set is first fully present in the reconstructed working tree — and post-prefix share of turns (all 9 models) and CoT chars (DS/KM), split into test-running vs pure reasoning. Add, on the same runs: tokens_per_action and steps ([TTI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07976)), tool productivity ([OTC-PO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.14870)), redundant-command share ([AKBE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26952)), void turns ([SimpleTIR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.02479)), turns-after-first-pass ([Tokenmaxxing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22807)), and the constructive/reflective marker mix from the 32-marker segmenter ([Beyond the Last Answer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.20708)). Predictions: solved-run post-prefix share v3.2 > v4-pro and o3 > gpt-5.5, k2.6 ≈ k3; tokens_per_action falls with generation while steps hold or rise; failures show either no prefix reached (lost) or a long post-prefix tail (grind); v3.2's cap-hit runs split into those two classes. Anchor the OA arm against the lab's own −22% output tokens / −45% tool calls for GPT-5 vs o3. Null: compression is uniform across pre- and post-prefix segments and tokens_per_action is flat. Note explicitly that the commitment prefix is *not* the published k*, which measures when the model already knew the answer — the two can diverge exactly in the r1 case, which is why the prefill probe is pre-registered.

**H2 — Action-grounded verification and silent faults separate r1's stop from v4's stop.** From commands and outputs alone: verification-claim sentences in CoT per executed test command (DS/KM; our unpublished ratio, §5a), premature_stop and unsupported_claim flags ([trajectory-judge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00038) rules), Unsupported Completion Claim and Search Loop ([TraceProbe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06184)), reasoning chars per command and longest reasoning-only streak ([Overthinking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08235) proxies; [Ada](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08500) cognitive streak; [Agent Genome](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15579) P-ratio), E→V transition probability, and the fabricated-output detector (claimed test output string-matched against actual outputs, [Fragility across languages](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27901) HU-Proc analogue) plus Restoration Errors ([CoT in the Wild](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.08679)). Predictions: r1 maximal on claim-per-test ratio, P-ratio and streak length with 12/12 `completed` exits; v3.2 maximal on Search Loop and duplicate-output rate; g3 low on both; within model, silent-fault flags on solved runs decrease g2→g3 (D1's clean-compression test). Nulls: claim-per-test ratio does not differ by generation once volume is normalised (the length-adjustment gate is doing the work), and — the stronger null — the CoT adds nothing over actions, i.e. the ACT-only classifier of GRADE matches the CoT+ACT one, consistent with 77% of behaviourally important events being action-visible ([Terminal Wrench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00583)) and with the ~0.6 AUROC text ceiling ([Hidden Error Awareness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09502)).

**H3 — Concentration: separate outcome from path, and test the rivals rather than the trend.** Layer A: per cell compute τ_div / first-divergence turn, unique command sequences, composition (verb-histogram JSD) and ordering (GAK) consistency, ProcGrep repertoire and compression ratio, FSM state count, **outcome-entropy** (distinct PatchDiff-equivalence classes among solved runs) and **path-entropy** (distinct command-sequence classes). Layer B: ReasonOps operator histograms; within-cell JSD vs cross-task JSD margin (DS/KM); plan–action agreement (our UAR analogue). Then evaluate the rival table: M1 predicts outcome-entropy collapses before path-entropy — the k2-thinking "forks in turn 1, byte-identical patch" profile, which is exactly [Invisible Leash](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.14843)'s local-stochasticity-without-global-exploration; M2/IPS predict the converged patch is also modal across labs (compute cross-lab modal share as the denominator — findings-verification already noted 11/30 external runs share the modal edit set on one task); M3/M5 predict v4-pro's modal patch coincides with v3.2's modal patch; M4 predicts style continuity within lineage; M6 predicts concentration tracks the localisation-probe hit rate; M7 predicts concentration tracks solvability, so everything is reported conditional on solved pairs with the consistency–correctness gap shown; M8 predicts non-monotonicity, so the Kimi V-shape is not evidence against RLVR sharpening. Predictions on the CoT side are deliberately weak and two-sided: the operator-JSD margin may rise with generation in step with the action-side margin, **or** fall, because higher-capability models are less monitorable (r = −0.87 to −0.39, [MonitorBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28590)) and verbalization scales with length. Null: CoT operator profiles are task-invariant style (cross-task JSD ≈ within-cell JSD) at every generation, in which case D2 is action-only and reasoning content carries no procedure signal.

## 5. Gaps

### 5a. Pattern families with no applicable literature

- **Cross-turn reasoning in an agentic loop.** Every CoT taxonomy in §1a was built on single-turn math/science/QA traces; none defines how a reasoning episode spans a tool observation (does a Verify episode close when the test output arrives, or when the model reads it?). Closest constructs: [Strained Coherence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07889) (one reasoning step → next action), [TACT](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.05980) (per-step OT/OA), [Act·onomy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13625) (reads text and tool calls), [SSA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17454) (intent-execution gap), [ARPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.19849) (uncertainty concentrated after tool feedback). Round-1 called this a total gap; the agentic-RL literature partially fills it at the *turn* level (void turns, thinking–acting gap, post-tool entropy) but **still supplies no episode/DAG scheme that crosses a tool boundary**. Our Implement→command and reflection→next-command groundings remain our own extensions.
- **Reasoning-to-action conversion.** No named construct measures the rate at which planned actions in CoT are executed. Nearest: overthinking score ([Danger of Overthinking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08235)), cognitive streak ([Ada](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08500)), P-ratio ([Agent Genome](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15579)), Thinking–Acting Gap ([AXPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28774)), text-terminating Plan edges (our ReasoningFlow extension).
- **Within-lab generational lineage on CoT content.** No paper follows one lab's models across generations on reasoning traces under a fixed harness. Generational evidence is action-side ([Behavioral Drivers](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.02547v1) Claude 3.5→opus-4.5; [SWE-EVO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18470) o3→gpt-5; [ProcGrep](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16988) release periods; [Reliability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16666) ~24 months) or on plain outputs ([Self-convergence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12683)). Specifically absent: any CoT comparison of r1-0528 → V3.2 → V4 or K2-thinking → K2.6 → K3, though members appear individually — r1-0528: [ReasonOps](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29192), [Long-CoT SFT patterns](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01702), [Societies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10825), [Monitorability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18311), [Curriculum RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22317); V3.2: [TraceGraph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.31308), [Ada](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08500), [Agent Tests](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07900), [AgentProcessBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14465), [Classifier Sensitivity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20172); V4 Pro/Flash: [FACE-Eval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29464), [TraceGraph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.31308) (continuation provider); K2-thinking: [ReasonOps](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29192), [AgentEcho](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21255), [Agent Tests](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07900), [Illegible](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.27338), [Implicit-Influence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04735), [Monitorability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18311); K2.6: [FACE-Eval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29464), [SWE-Marathon](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.07682v1), [TACT](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.05980) (as judge); gpt-5.5: [SWE-Marathon](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.07682v1), [Tokenmaxxing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22807), [Reliability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16666); o1: [Danger of Overthinking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08235), [ThinkARM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.19995) (o1-mini), [Reliability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16666); o3: [SWE-EVO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18470), [Monitorability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18311), [HAL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.11977), [Hodoscope](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11072), [Anti-scheming](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.15541).
- **Frontier specialists-then-distill effects on CoT patterns.** Distillation evidence is on small open distills ([Bloom's](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23205), [NoWait](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.08343), [CoT-FSM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.22437), [Performative CoT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11746), [EOPD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07079)), a LoRA student ([AgentEcho](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21255)), SWE-agent-LM-32B ([ProcGrep](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16988)), one 7B model family ([Where diversity collapses](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.16027v1)), or theory ([Provable RLVR](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.22938v1)). [MOPD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30406) is now the closest published recipe to V4/K3 (per-domain RL experts incl. SWE, multi-teacher on-policy distillation, entropy tracked) — but it is Qwen3-30B-A3B / MiMo-V2-Flash, reports **no** diversity or pass@k metric, and analyses no traces. **Nothing** covers multi-effort-level teacher mixtures on CoT patterns; the K3 multimodal-length conjecture is ours.
- **Mechanism choice relative to gold (D6).** No reasoning-content construct describes *why* a model chooses a different fix mechanism; the literature gives scope/collateral codes ([Fail Their Users](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29442), [Wink](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17037)) and behavioural equivalence classes ([PatchDiff](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.15223)) only. The one encouraging opening is that code design decisions appear far better verbalized than preferences (~93% detection in the coding setting, [Implicit-Influence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04735)) — untested on agentic repository work.
- **Style vs task-directed procedure decomposition.** The two-component reading in findings-verification has no literature counterpart; [Same Signal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18332) separates framework from LLM, and [How Consistent](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.28840) separates structure from arguments, but neither separates style from policy.
- **Hallucinated verification as a measured rate.** Only qualitative in [NL2Repo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12730) and interpretive there; our claim-per-test ratio is unpublished. The nearest published measurables are procedural exploitation ([Fragility across languages](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27901)) and Unsupported Completion Claim ([TraceProbe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06184)).
- **Hierarchical / subgoal nesting in agentic CoT.** Cognitive Foundations names Hierarchical organization but on non-agentic traces; no coding rule for nesting across tool calls exists.
- **Human developer comparator for agentic procedure.** Round 1 claimed "no human comparator" as a channel limit; the accurate version is narrower. Cognitive Foundations (54 think-aloud traces), [Anatomy of R1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07410) (human competition solutions) and [Schoenfeld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14662) (human-aligned transitions) anchor *math* reasoning against humans, and Act·onomy/TraceProbe milestones are human-authored codebooks — but **no verified record in this map contains human developer debugging traces on SWE-bench-style tasks**. Human program-comprehension and debugging literature (think-aloud, IDE telemetry, information foraging) was not searched and is not represented here, so any "human-like procedure" anchoring for D2 remains unavailable *from these records*; naming it as a searchable gap is the honest position.
- **API-served model version drift.** No verified paper here measures behaviour drift of a fixed API model name over time; §4b item 12 handles it by documentation only.

### 5b. Channels the literature cannot cover

- **OpenAI reasoning summaries.** Excluded as CoT by [Monitoring Monitorability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18311); the only fidelity study is on **model-written** self-summaries of open models ([Observability Ladder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02089v1)), where summaries add only +0.019 AUROC over the response once the prompt is visible and Last90 matches them at equal length, and where much of the linear trace signal is length. [Anti-scheming](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.15541) shows what the o-series raw CoT contains that summaries cannot preserve (dialect, CoT–action contradictions, causally load-bearing evaluation awareness). Consequence: every CoT-side construct in §2 is DS/KM-only unless marked action-anchored presence-level. **Still unmeasured anywhere:** the fidelity of provider-generated summaries themselves (summary length vs billed reasoning tokens, information retained, any comparison against raw CoT under a research-access program).
- **Raw CoT as a readout at all.** Verbalization of decision-relevant facts is 25–39% in the best-studied setting and <2% for exploit-type decisions, and is lowest exactly on the tool-return channel our evidence arrives through ([Anthropic](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.05410); [FACE-Eval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29464)). A reasoning rewrite that leaves commands byte-identical collapses monitor recall from ~95% to 4–11% on the action-invisible subset ([Terminal Wrench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00583)). Faithfulness measured on identical traces spans 69.7–82.6% by classifier choice ([Classifier Sensitivity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20172)). Bound: **presence is evidence, absence is not**, and no CoT-derived rate should be reported as a point estimate.
- **No logprobs / activations.** Rules out [CoDE-Stop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04930), RCPD's `</think>` rank ([Evolution of Thought](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.17627)), [Performative CoT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11746), [Base Models Know How](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07364), [Hidden Error Awareness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09502), CREST steering, Thought Anchors attribution, Societies' SAE feature, [TRUST](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06976) perplexity margins, [ARPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.19849)/[AEPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.14545) token-entropy constructs, [Invisible Leash](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.14843)'s token-vs-answer entropy decomposition, and all weight-space work ([small subnetworks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.11711), [Path Not Taken](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08567)). Black-box proxies exist for some (patch stability, verbalised certainty, post-output lexical novelty, nearest-neighbour text distance).
- **No prefill / no resampling from a prefix.** Blocks the true critical prefix k* ([How Much Thinking Is Enough](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23926)), counterfactual anchor importance ([Thought Anchors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.19143)), the causal-relevance eval of [Korbak et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.11473), and the reasoning-rewrite stress test ([Terminal Wrench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00583)). DeepSeek/Kimi prefill would enable the first two on a subset — pre-registered as future instrumentation.
- **Cross-host sampling regimes and version drift.** The DS g2→g3 and KM g1→g2 links cross serving hosts with unrecorded sampling defaults; [Reliability without Validity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19544) and [Consistency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10516) require T=0 replication or known perturbations we cannot reconstruct, and temperature choice itself moves variance between components ([Adding Error Bars](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00640)). OA is the only host-clean lineage. Version drift is undocumented in this literature.
- **K=3 repeats.** Too few for U-statistics / MMD ([Consistency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10516)), mode accuracy and leakage ([Optimal Reasoning Length](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09591)), Min-kNN at k=10 ([Detecting RLVR Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11792)), reasoning-graph decay fits (K=2000 clusters, [RL Squeezes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.21128)), cross-trace variance F-tests ([Anatomy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07410)), and Vendi-style effective-mode counts. At K=3 a task solved once is already saturated for pass@k ([Overtraining](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.15455v1)), and single-run pass@1 spreads 2.2–6.0 pp ([On Randomness](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.07150v2)).
- **Four task clusters, effectively ~2.** Difficulty-conditioned constructs ([When More Thinking Hurts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10739), ThinkARM problem-type effects, Cognitive Foundations' ill-structured contrast) need the expansion; and with 3 sympy + 1 pytest the effective independent-sample count is smaller than 4 ([BHM-ESC](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.05709v2)), while ~969 questions would be needed for a 3-pp pass@1 contrast ([Adding Error Bars](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00640)).
- **Cost- and turn-cap censoring.** Kimi cost caps and v3.2's 100-turn cap make post-solution-tail, redundancy and burst-position metrics lower bounds; budget pressure restructures trajectories model-specifically ([Ada](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08500)), and masking cap-hit trajectories is itself known to hide looping ([Nebius](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.03501)) — caps are not uniform truncation.
- **Grading validity.** GRADE is not a clean label: 7.8% of "solved" Verified patches fail the full developer suite and ~11% of plausible patches are estimated incorrect, inflating resolution rates by ~6.2 pp ([PatchDiff](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.15223)); reward hacking and test tampering are live for the expansion ([Hodoscope](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11072), [SWE-Marathon](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.07682v1), [HAL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.11977), [Baker et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.11926)).
- **Contamination is not falsifiable with our instruments.** Behavioural probes (localisation, function reproduction, session-isolated diversity, patch-similarity lumps) are the only admissible covariates; log-prob detectors collapse after GRPO and we have no logprobs regardless ([Fragility of Contamination Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02386)). Pre-register that a null is not evidence of no contamination.
